# Privacy Policy for Twitch Mute Shouts

**Effective date:** 2026-10-03

Twitch Mute Shouts does **not** collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

## What this extension does

This extension injects a small CSS rule on `*.twitch.tv` pages to hide the unread badge on Twitch's Shouts button.

## Data collection and transmission

- No personal information is collected.
- No browsing history is collected.
- No analytics or telemetry is collected.
- No cookies are read or written by the extension.
- No data is sent to any server.
- No third parties receive any data from this extension.

## Permissions and scope

The extension runs only on Twitch domains (`*://*.twitch.tv/*`) for its single visual UI change.

## Changes to this policy

If data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before release of that change.
